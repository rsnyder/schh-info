## At a glance

The company holds an active South Carolina Contractor's Licensing Board Mechanical Contractor license (#109588, Electrical classification EL4, expiring 10/31/2027) with owner Keith A. Fisher as the license's qualifying party, verified on the official LLR lookup on 2026-08-24 ([SC LLR Contractors lookup](https://verify.llronline.com/LicLookup/Contractors/Contractor.aspx?div=69)). ## Credentials, registrations, and official records

Which licenses apply: In South Carolina, residential electrical work over $500 is regulated by the LLR Residential Builders Commission, and commercial electrical work over $10,000 requires a Contractor's Licensing Board Mechanical Contractor license with the Electrical (EL) classification ([LLR Residential Electrician page](https://www.llr.sc.gov/res/reselectric.aspx); [LLR CLB licensure page](https://llr.sc.gov/clb/clb_licensure.aspx)). The Contractor's Licensing Board lookup page states that commercial contractors "may also perform residential work."

- **SC Contractor's Licensing Board — verified 2026-08-24 (interactive lookup):** Bluffton Electric LLC, license #109588, type Mechanical Contractor, status ACTIVE, first issued 05/04/2007, expires 10/31/2027, classification Electrical-EL4, qualified by financial statement, President/Owner Keith A. Fisher ([SC LLR Contractors lookup](https://verify.llronline.com/LicLookup/Contractors/Contractor.aspx?div=69) — search "Bluffton Electric"). "EL4" means electrical work up to $400,000 per job or bid ([LLR classification definitions](https://llr.sc.gov/clb/PDFFiles/CLBClassificationAbbreviations.pdf)).
- **Qualifying party:** The license record lists Keith Fisher as the supervising qualifying party; his LLR qualifier record (#24082) shows "MECHANICAL Primary Qualifying Party," status ACTIVE, classification EL. In plain terms, the state license belongs to the company and is backed by Mr. Fisher's electrical qualification.
- **Board public-action lookup:** The public board-order lookup displayed no orders for this license on 2026-08-24. This statement is limited to what the lookup displayed; it does not address complaints, investigations, or non-public matters.

## Questions to ask

1. Will the work be performed under SC Contractor's Licensing Board license #109588, and will a licensed electrician supervise the crew on site?
2. Can you provide current certificates of general liability and workers' compensation insurance naming me as certificate holder, and details of the bond you reference?
3. Is there a service-call or diagnostic fee, and is it credited toward the job if I proceed?
4. Will I receive a written estimate, and how is additional work authorized and priced if something unexpected is found?
5. Will you pull the Town of Bluffton or Beaufort County electrical permit and schedule the inspection, and is that included in the price?
6. What warranty do you offer on labor, and what manufacturer warranty applies to parts or a generator?
7. For a generator: are you an authorized Generac dealer for warranty service, who performs maintenance afterward, and what does an annual maintenance plan cost?
8. Do you use subcontractors for any part of the job (for example, gas or concrete work for a generator)?
9. What is your typical lead time for scheduling in Sun City, and what does the after-hours emergency line cover and cost?
10. What payment methods do you accept, is a deposit required, and when is final payment due?
