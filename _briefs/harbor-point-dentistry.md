## At a glance

Harbor Point Dentistry is a single-dentist general dental practice in the Okatie Center medical/office complex on Okatie Center Boulevard South (ZIP 29909, the same ZIP as Sun City Hilton Head). It is owned and operated by Dr. Katherine Elizabeth Abell, DMD, who practices publicly as "Dr. Elizabeth Abell." Her South Carolina dental license (No. 8285, General Dentist) was verified Active on 2026-08-24 with an expiration of 03/01/2027, and the practice's LLC has been on file with the South Carolina Secretary of State since 2016 in Good Standing. The practice describes itself as offering general, restorative, aesthetic, periodontal, root canal, extraction, TMJ, emergency, and oral sleep-appliance services. It also markets its sleep-appliance work under the name "Dental Sleep Medicine of the Lowcountry." The office is open Monday through Thursday.

## Services and operating model

All service descriptions below are practice-reported from the [practice website](https://www.harborpointdentistry.com/) (retrieved 2026-08-24, pages undated) unless noted.

- **Services listed:** implant restoration, aesthetic dentistry, comprehensive care, periodontal (gum) treatments, root canal treatment, extractions, TMJ (jaw-joint) disorder relief, sleep disorder appliances, basic restorative dentistry (fillings, crowns), and emergency appointments.
- **What is and is not stated about in-house vs. referred care:**
  - *Implants:* the website describes implant **restoration** (the crown or denture that attaches to an implant) and candidacy. It does not say who surgically places the implant post or whether placement is done in this office or by an outside surgeon. Ask.
  - *Root canals:* the website says Dr. Abell and staff provide root canal treatment. It does not describe which cases, if any, are referred to an endodontist (root-canal specialist).
  - *Extractions:* described as done with "highly effective anesthetics" (local numbing). No statement about surgical or wisdom-tooth extractions, or referral to an oral surgeon.
  - *Sedation:* the website does not describe sedation options. The state license record shows "Permitted for Sedation: N" (see Credentials). That field is reported literally; it does not tell you what anxiety or comfort measures the office does offer.
- **Sleep appliances:** the practice's sleep site states that after a patient has been diagnosed with sleep apnea or sleep-disordered breathing, it "will coordinate with your physician" to determine whether an oral appliance is appropriate, presented as an option when CPAP is not workable ([sleepbluffton.com](https://www.sleepbluffton.com/), retrieved 2026-08-24). The site does not say whether it bills medical insurance or Medicare for these devices.
- **Dental laboratory:** the website states all lab work is performed in the U.S. by partner labs.
- **Hours:** Website: Monday-Thursday 8 am-5 pm, Friday closed. Yelp lists 8:30 am-5 pm Monday-Thursday; PatientConnect365 lists Friday "by appointment." Confirm by phone.
- **Appointments:** phone, website contact form, or the online scheduling link on the website. New-patient process, emergency after-hours procedure, and accessibility accommodations are not described on the website.
- **Languages:** a third-party directory lists English and Spanish ([PatientConnect365](https://www.patientconnect365.com/Dentists/SouthCarolina/Okatie/29909/Harbor_Point_Dentistry), retrieved 2026-08-24; unverified).
- **Insurance and payment:** the website's insurance page says only that the office helps patients understand their dental insurance plan and names an insurance coordinator; it publishes no list of in-network plans, membership plan, financing program, deposit, cancellation, or refund terms ([insurance page](https://www.harborpointdentistry.com/insurance), retrieved 2026-08-24). PatientConnect365 reports "accepts most major health plans" and cash, check, Mastercard, and Visa (third-party, unverified). A CareCredit locator page exists for the practice but could not be opened, so financing availability is not verified.
- **Medicare:** Original Medicare generally does not cover routine dental care (cleanings, fillings, crowns, dentures). Some Medicare Advantage plans include dental benefits; Original Medicare may cover a physician-ordered oral appliance for diagnosed sleep apnea only through an enrolled supplier. No source stated whether this practice is enrolled with Medicare for any purpose. Not verified; ask directly.

## Important considerations

- The office is closed Fridays (website), so emergency coverage on Fridays and weekends should be asked about specifically.
- "Permitted for Sedation: N" on the state license means the licensee does not hold a state sedation permit. If you need sedation beyond local anesthesia or nitrous-type comfort measures, ask what is offered and by whom.
- The website does not say whether implant placement, surgical extractions, or complex root canals are done in this office or referred to a specialist; residents who want single-office care should ask.
- No in-network insurance list, membership plan, or financing terms are published; the CareCredit listing could not be verified.
- Hours differ slightly between the website and third-party listings.
- Medicare coverage for dental work is limited; do not assume coverage for any service, including sleep appliances, without written confirmation.

## Questions to ask

1. Will Dr. Abell be my dentist of record for every visit, and are there other dentists or hygienists who may see me?
2. For implants, who places the implant post — this office or an outside oral surgeon or periodontist — and who handles the restoration?
3. Which root canals and extractions are done here, and when do you refer to an endodontist or oral surgeon?
4. What comfort or anxiety options are available, given that the state license does not include a sedation permit?
5. Which dental insurance plans are you in-network with today, and will you submit a pre-treatment estimate to my plan?
6. Do you offer an in-office membership plan, CareCredit, or other financing, and what are the deposit, cancellation, and refund policies?
7. Do you bill medical insurance or Medicare for oral sleep appliances, and what physician documentation is required first?
8. What happens if I have a dental emergency on a Friday, weekend, or evening?
9. Is the Suite 302 office accessible for walkers or wheelchairs, and is there help with transfers into the chair?
10. Will you provide a written treatment plan with alternatives and itemized costs before work begins, and how are records transferred if I move practices?
