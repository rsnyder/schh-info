## At a glance

Novant Health New Riverside Primary Care is a primary-care clinic that Novant Health opened in December 2025 in the New Riverside Village area of Bluffton, off May River Road, at 375 Hope Pond Way, Unit 107 ([Novant clinic page](https://www.novanthealth.org/locations/clinics/new-riverside-primary-care/), retrieved 2026-08-24; [Novant newsroom, Oct. 1, 2025](https://www.novanthealth.org/newsroom/novant-health-marks-primary-care-expansion-with-new-clinics-eight-new-lowcountry-providers); [Bluffton Chamber ribbon cutting, Dec. 4, 2025](https://blufftonchamberofcommerce.org/events/details/ribbon-cutting-celebration-for-novant-health-new-riverside-primary-care-19614)). Novant lists three clinicians there: two internal-medicine physicians, William Kyle, DO, and Kyle McMillen, DO, and a family nurse practitioner, Tore Flavin, FNP ([clinic page](https://www.novanthealth.org/locations/clinics/new-riverside-primary-care/)). All three hold active South Carolina licenses, confirmed in the state LLR lookup on 2026-08-24, and all three are shown by Novant as accepting new patients on that date. The clinic describes itself as serving patients of all ages, with same-day and next-day sick visits, physicals, vaccinations and chronic-condition care (Novant-reported). Medicare details are mixed in the official data and should be confirmed with the office before scheduling (see below).

## Important considerations

- **New clinic, records in transition.** Federal NPI, state license and Medicare enrollment records for all three clinicians still show earlier employers or addresses. Identity is not in doubt, but Medicare and insurance directories may not yet list them at this location.
- **Medicare assignment is not uniform in the official data** (see above). Ask specifically whether the clinician you will see accepts Original Medicare assignment.
- **Clinician backgrounds, from the state records.** Dr. McMillen's permanent South Carolina license was issued in October 2025, following a 2022 training license; Ms. Flavin has held her nurse-practitioner license since 2021 and works under a collaborating-physician arrangement with Dr. McMillen, a standard structure in South Carolina. Ask who your clinician-of-record will be.
- **Nurse practitioner vs. physician.** A family nurse practitioner is a licensed advanced-practice nurse who can diagnose, treat and prescribe within South Carolina's rules; a DO is a fully licensed physician. Choose based on your preference and ask how coverage works between them.
- **Board certification:** verified through the ABMS public lookup for Dr. Kyle; no record displayed for Dr. McMillen; not checkable from public sources for Ms. Flavin (see Credentials).
- **Hours, telehealth, after-hours coverage and pricing policies are not published** on the pages reviewed.
- **Not the same as Novant's other Bluffton projects.** Novant's planned 50-bed Bluffton hospital (expected 2028) and its Washington Square emergency department are separate facilities ([Novant newsroom](https://www.novanthealth.org/newsroom/s-c-approves-novant-healths-certificate-of-need-application-for-new-50-bed-hospital-in-bluffton)).

## Questions to ask

1. Which clinician would be my primary clinician-of-record, and is that person accepting new patients today?
2. Does that clinician accept Original Medicare assignment, and is the practice's Medicare enrollment at this Hope Pond Way address complete?
3. Are you in-network for my Medicare Advantage plan or my Medigap/supplement arrangement? Can you confirm in writing?
4. What is the current wait for a new-patient appointment, and is the Medicare Annual Wellness Visit offered?
5. What are the clinic's hours, and who covers after-hours or urgent questions? Which emergency department or urgent care do you direct patients to?
6. If I need to be admitted, will it be to Novant Health Hilton Head Medical Center or Coastal Carolina Medical Center (the two hospitals CMS records associate with the physicians), who admits me — my physician or a hospitalist — and how are hospital follow-ups handled?
7. How are referrals to specialists and prescription renewals handled, and how quickly are portal messages answered?
8. Are video or phone visits available, and for what kinds of concerns?
9. If a nurse practitioner sees me, when would I see a physician, and who supervises or collaborates?
10. What happens if my clinician leaves the practice; will my records and appointments transfer within Novant?

## Research limitations

- Board certification could be verified only where a certifying board publishes a free public lookup (ABMS, for Dr. Kyle). Osteopathic boards and the nurse-practitioner certifying bodies do not, so Dr. McMillen's status is "no record displayed" and Ms. Flavin's certifying body is unconfirmed.
- Medicare participation and assignment were read from the CMS clinician dataset on 2026-08-24; the dataset lags employment changes and does not yet show a Novant group at this address. Medicare Advantage plan participation was not researched.
- New-patient acceptance is Novant-reported as of 2026-08-24 and can change at any time.
- Clinic hours, telehealth availability, after-hours coverage, pricing and cancellation policies were not published on the pages reviewed.
- The exact South Carolina legal entity that bills for this clinic was not confirmed from a corporate filing.
- "No Orders Found" in the state board lookups covers only public board orders displayed on 2026-08-24; it does not establish the absence of complaints, investigations or other matters, and this brief does not assess the quality of any clinician's care.
- Independent review evidence is too new and too small to support practice-level conclusions.
