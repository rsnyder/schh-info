## What you can do

Ask ChatGPT questions about information available in the Community Guide, then inspect the supporting source passages. Topics include community amenities, publications, rules, and events. Coverage depends on the material currently available in the Guide.

Try questions such as:

- What hours are listed for the indoor pools? Show the source.
- What does the Guide say about preparing for hurricane season?
- Find the supporting passage for that answer.

The connection offers two capabilities: ask the Guide and retrieve a Guide source. It does not submit vendor recommendations, create contribution drafts, send messages to administrators, or change community records.

## Who can connect

Access requires a Guide account and verified SCHH resident authorization. Sign in on the Guide's sign-in page, complete any required email verification there, and authorize the connection. The Guide checks your authorization when you use its tools and may recheck your eligibility.

Do not enter passwords or email verification codes into a ChatGPT conversation. Original community-portal links may require a separate portal sign-in; connecting the Guide does not grant access to that portal.

An isolated reviewer account uses clearly labeled, fictional sample documents. Those examples are for testing and are not community information.

## Availability

The ChatGPT integration is in development. A public directory listing is not yet available. This page does not indicate OpenAI approval or endorsement. Residents can continue to use the [Community Guide website](https://schh.info/).

## Check important answers

AI-generated answers can be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. Check the cited document's date and the original source before acting on schedules, fees, rules, or safety information. If the Guide cannot find supporting evidence, it may be unable to answer.

For emergencies, call 911. Use official community and public-agency channels for urgent notices and current safety instructions.
